- President of the [Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Society at the Queen Mary University of London](http://qmrs.co.uk) for two years.
- Winner of MEng Project of the year https://www.sems.qmul.ac.uk/news/5267/robot-hand-team-wins-top-prize-at-ilf
